Media component · specimens

Component sheet for review, not a product screen. Specimen images are neutral guides; Videogo images are demo fixtures (no real media exists in the repositories), labelled once per instance. Fixture illustration style is not Videogo’s brand style. Crop mode is computed by js/media.js from each image’s focal point, subject box and title box.

1 · Source ratios → 4:5 card crop

Original shown whole (left of each pair) and as the 4:5 Studio card crop (right). Cover crop is used only when the subject and on-screen title stay inside the window; otherwise the card shows the full frame on a neutral mat and says so.

2 · Media types and provenance

Video poster frame with format/channel and duration; image preview identified as a source photo; provenance always visible on fixtures.

3 · Media states

Available real image or poster frame · Rendering reserved media area with an explicit state · Unavailable bounded “Preview unavailable” (media exists but cannot be shown; product trigger TBD) · Absent compact text, no media rectangle. Then selected and keyboard focus on an available card.

4 · Responsive card widths

The 4:5 crop scales with the card; chips and provenance stay fixed size. 154px ≈ 1216 viewport · 189px ≈ 1440 viewport · 280px ≈ two-column reflow.
